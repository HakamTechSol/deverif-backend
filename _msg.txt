HR Letters: org letterhead logo, diagonal REVOKED watermark, revoked->draft

Three fixes to the letter lifecycle.

Header logo. generateHrLetterPdf now takes an organizationLogoPath and uses it
in place of the platform mark. The letterhead is what makes the document
recognisably the employer's, and every letter previously carried the Dverif
logo instead. Falls back to the platform logo when an organization has none, and
a missing or corrupt file degrades to the fallback rather than failing issuance.
Resolution follows certificatePdf.js: basename() is load-bearing, because the
stored value is a relative path and joining it verbatim would let a value like
"../../.env" escape ORGS_DIR.

The column is organizations.logo, not logo_path.

REVOKED watermark. Was a solid red badge painted over the header, which covered
the logo and left the rest of the page looking like a valid letter. Now a
-30 degree REVOKED at 0.13 opacity across the centre of every page, drawn last so
it sits over the content. fillOpacity rather than a pale colour, so the text
underneath shows through. Rotation is applied around the page centre and undone,
and the transform is applied per page so a multi-page body cannot leak it.

Reverting a revocation. Revoking was a one-way door: a letter revoked over a
typo could be neither edited into a correct draft nor issued, so HR had to
re-create it and lose the reference number and audit trail.
POST /org/hr-letters/:uuid/re-draft returns it to draft, clearing
qr_token/qr_signature/issued_at/revoked_at/revoked_reason/body_snapshot -
exactly the inverse of revoke, plus the body so a corrected template takes
effect.

It does NOT re-issue. status becomes draft and nothing else; setting issued_at
here would put a letter back into circulation on one click without anyone
re-reading it, which is what revoking exists to prevent. Issuing stays a
separate step with its own preview and merge-tag collection.

Status-guarded to revoked only, so a double submit cannot discard an issued
letter's attestation.

Verified by decoding the emitted PDF operators rather than by eye: a revoked
render carries one more rotation transform and one more text-showing operator
than the same letter issued, and the org-logo render differs byte-for-byte from
the fallback.
